aka: short names for long shell commands

Short names for long commands. One file, every shell: bash, zsh, fish and PowerShell.

$ curl -fsSL https://github.com/boltbench/aka/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
aka add
Name any command once. It works right away, in the terminal you're in and every new one.
aka suggest
Reads your history, locally, and finds the long commands worth shortening.
aka undo
Every change is backed up. Nothing gets replaced without asking.
AGENTS.md
List your aliases for your AI agent and it writes glog instead of the whole thing. Fewer characters, fewer tokens.
